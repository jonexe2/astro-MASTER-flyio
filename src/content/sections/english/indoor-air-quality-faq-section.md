title: "Frequently Asked Questions About Indoor Air Quality"

items:
  - question: "What does indoor air quality mean?"
    answer: "Indoor air quality describes conditions that affect the air inside a home, including airborne particles, filtration, humidity, ventilation, and HVAC airflow. The concerns and appropriate solutions can differ from one home to another."
  - question: "What filtration options are available for an HVAC system?"
    answer: "Options can range from compatible replacement filters to upgraded whole-home filtration equipment. Selection should account for the HVAC system and its airflow requirements, because a filter must be appropriate for the equipment it serves."
  - question: "How can indoor humidity affect home comfort?"
    answer: "Humidity that is too high or too low can make indoor conditions feel uncomfortable and may contribute to moisture-related concerns. HVAC-integrated humidification or dehumidification may be appropriate depending on the home, season, and existing equipment."
  - question: "Can indoor air quality solutions work with my existing HVAC system?"
    answer: "Many filtration, humidity-control, and air-cleaning options are designed to integrate with existing HVAC equipment, but compatibility depends on system configuration, available space, controls, and airflow requirements. An evaluation helps narrow down suitable options."
  - question: "How do I choose the right indoor air quality solution?"
    answer: "Start with the specific concerns in your home, then review the existing HVAC system, filtration, humidity conditions, and airflow. A system-based evaluation can help compare practical options without assuming that one product is right for every home."
