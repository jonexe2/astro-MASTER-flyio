title: "Frequently Asked Questions About HVAC Maintenance"

items:
  - question: "How often is HVAC maintenance typically scheduled?"
    answer: "Many homeowners schedule maintenance around seasonal operation, with cooling equipment checked before warmer weather and heating equipment checked before colder weather. The appropriate schedule can vary based on system type, age, usage, and manufacturer guidance."
  - question: "Why does routine HVAC maintenance matter?"
    answer: "Maintenance provides an opportunity to inspect system condition, check operation, review airflow and controls, and identify developing concerns. It can support seasonal readiness, although it cannot guarantee that equipment will never experience a breakdown."
  - question: "What may be included in an HVAC maintenance visit?"
    answer: "The visit may include inspection of relevant components, operating-condition checks, airflow and control review, and evaluation of visible wear or cleanliness concerns. The exact work depends on the equipment and the maintenance scope."
  - question: "Do both heating and cooling systems need maintenance?"
    answer: "Heating and cooling equipment each operate under different seasonal demands and may have different maintenance needs. Homes with separate systems may benefit from having each checked before its primary season, while heat pumps may be reviewed with year-round operation in mind."
  - question: "Can maintenance help identify developing HVAC problems?"
    answer: "A maintenance visit can reveal wear, changes in operation, airflow concerns, or other conditions that may need attention. Finding a concern early can provide more time to consider the next step, but maintenance does not prevent every possible repair."
