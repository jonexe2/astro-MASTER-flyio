title: "Frequently Asked Questions"

items:
  - question: "How often should I schedule HVAC maintenance?"
    answer: "We recommend scheduling professional maintenance twice a year — once in the spring for your cooling system and once in the fall for your heating system. Regular tune-ups keep equipment running efficiently, catch small issues before they become costly repairs, and help extend the lifespan of your unit."
  - question: "What size HVAC system do I need for my home?"
    answer: "System sizing depends on your home's square footage, ceiling height, insulation, number of windows, and local climate. A unit that is too large will short-cycle and fail to dehumidify, while one that is too small will run constantly and struggle to reach the set temperature. Our technicians perform a full load calculation to recommend the right capacity for your space."
  - question: "How can I improve my HVAC system's energy efficiency?"
    answer: "Simple steps like replacing filters every one to three months, sealing leaky ductwork, installing a programmable thermostat, and keeping outdoor units clear of debris can noticeably lower energy bills. Pairing these habits with biannual professional maintenance keeps the system running at peak efficiency year-round."
  - question: "Why is my HVAC system making strange noises?"
    answer: "Banging, rattling, squealing, or grinding sounds usually point to a loose or worn component such as a blower wheel, motor bearing, or belt. Turn the system off and contact a technician to prevent further damage. Ignoring unusual noises often leads to more expensive repairs down the line."
  - question: "When should I replace my HVAC system?"
    answer: "Most systems last 10 to 15 years with proper care. If your unit needs frequent repairs, struggles to maintain comfort, or your energy bills keep climbing, replacement may be more cost-effective. Upgrading to a high-efficiency model can also qualify you for manufacturer rebates and lower monthly utility costs."
  - question: "Do you offer emergency HVAC repair services?"
    answer: "Yes. We provide 24/7 emergency repair for heating and cooling failures that threaten comfort or safety. Call our emergency line anytime and a certified technician will be dispatched to diagnose and stabilize your system as quickly as possible."
