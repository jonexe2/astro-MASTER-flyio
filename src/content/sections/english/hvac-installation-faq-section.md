  - question: "How much does HVAC installation cost in Boston?"
    answer: "The cost of a new HVAC system depends on the type of equipment, system size, efficiency level, existing ductwork, installation requirements, and the layout of the home. BlueFlame Heating & Air evaluates the property and reviews the available system options before providing a clear installation estimate."
  - question: "How long does HVAC installation take?"
    answer: "Many standard HVAC replacement projects can be completed within a day, while larger or more complex installations may require additional time. The schedule depends on the equipment being installed, accessibility, ductwork or electrical requirements, and the scope of the project. We review the expected timeline with you before installation begins."
  - question: "How long should a new HVAC system last?"
    answer: "System lifespan depends on the type of equipment, installation quality, usage, maintenance, and operating conditions. Many modern heating and cooling systems can provide reliable service for well over a decade when they are properly installed and maintained. Routine maintenance can also help protect performance and identify problems before they become larger repairs."
  - question: "Should I repair my current HVAC system or replace it?"
    answer: "Repair may make sense when the equipment is still in good overall condition and the problem is isolated. Replacement becomes more attractive when the system is older, repairs are becoming frequent, comfort is declining, or the equipment is no longer operating efficiently. We can evaluate the existing system and explain both options so you can make an informed decision."
  - question: "How do I know what size HVAC system my home needs?"
    answer: "HVAC equipment should be selected based on the home's actual heating and cooling requirements rather than square footage alone. Factors such as insulation, windows, layout, construction, ductwork, sun exposure, and local climate can all affect system sizing. Proper evaluation helps avoid equipment that is too large or too small for the home."
