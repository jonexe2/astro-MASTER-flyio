title: "Frequently Asked Questions About AC Repair"

items:
  - question: "What are the signs that my AC system may need repair?"
    answer: "Common signs include warm air, weak airflow, uneven cooling, frequent cycling, unusual noises, difficulty reaching the thermostat setting, or a system that will not start. Because several conditions can cause similar symptoms, an inspection is needed to identify the actual problem."
  - question: "Why is my AC running but not cooling the home?"
    answer: "The cause may involve airflow, controls, electrical components, equipment condition, or another issue affecting cooling performance. Checking that the thermostat is set correctly is a useful first step, but continued poor cooling generally calls for professional diagnosis."
  - question: "What can cause weak airflow from my vents?"
    answer: "Weak airflow may be related to filtration, duct conditions, the indoor equipment, controls, or other system factors. The appropriate repair depends on where airflow is being restricted or why the equipment is not moving air as expected."
  - question: "How long does an AC repair usually take?"
    answer: "Repair time depends on the diagnosed problem, equipment access, required work, and whether a needed part is available. Some repairs can be completed during one visit, while more involved conditions may require additional time or follow-up service."
  - question: "Should I repair or replace my air conditioner?"
    answer: "The decision depends on equipment age and condition, the type and cost of the repair, cooling performance, and how often service has been needed. An isolated repair may make sense for otherwise sound equipment, while recurring problems or declining performance may justify discussing replacement."
