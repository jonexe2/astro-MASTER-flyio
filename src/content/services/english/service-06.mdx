## HVAC Help When Your System Stops Working

Some heating and cooling problems require prompt professional attention. A loss of heat during cold weather, no cooling during hot weather, an unexpected system shutdown, a major drop in performance, or unusual equipment behavior can quickly affect comfort and may indicate a problem that should be evaluated.

BlueFlame Heating & Air helps Boston homeowners understand what is happening when an HVAC system stops working as expected. We inspect the equipment, evaluate system operation, and diagnose the conditions behind the problem before recommending a repair or another appropriate next step.

Urgency does not replace careful diagnosis. Identifying the actual cause helps avoid assumptions and provides a clearer basis for restoring heating or cooling safely and dependably.

## When an HVAC Problem Cannot Wait

An urgent HVAC problem may involve a complete loss of heating or cooling, repeated shutdowns, unusual noises, electrical symptoms, or another sudden change in operation. The right response depends on the equipment, weather conditions, household needs, and whether there are signs of an immediate safety concern.

Homeowners can check basic items such as thermostat settings and whether the system has power when it is safe to do so. If those checks do not restore operation, professional evaluation can determine what is preventing the equipment from working normally.

If you smell gas, a carbon monoxide alarm sounds, smoke or fire is present, or you suspect immediate danger, leave the affected area and contact the appropriate emergency or utility service. Do not remain in the area or attempt to troubleshoot the HVAC equipment yourself.
